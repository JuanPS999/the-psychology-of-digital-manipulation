# The Psychology of Digital Manipulation

A catalog of psychological manipulation techniques — initially based on Robert Cialdini's principles of persuasion — mapped to real-world cases of digital attacks and manipulation (phishing, dark patterns, social engineering, disinformation, and more).

## Ethical disclaimer

This repository exists for **defensive and educational purposes only**. Its goal is to help people recognize and resist manipulation techniques, by studying publicly documented, real-world incidents. It is not a manual for manipulating others. All cases referenced here are based on publicly available reporting and research.

## Structure

- [`principles/`](principles/) — Cialdini's seven principles of persuasion, one page each.
- [`techniques/`](techniques/) — other manipulation techniques beyond Cialdini (reactance, anchoring, FOMO…). Placeholder for future expansion.
- [`cases/`](cases/) — documented real-world cases. Includes a [case template](cases/_template.md).

## Linking convention (cases ↔ principles)

The relationship between cases and principles is many-to-many, and links are maintained **in both directions**:

1. Every case file starts with an **Active principles** line linking to the relevant principle pages.
2. Every principle page ends with a **Cases using this principle** section linking back to the relevant cases.

When adding or editing a case, update the affected principle pages as well.

## Languages

Content is currently written in **English**. A Portuguese version is planned for the future.
