# Social proof

**Cialdini principle #6** — In uncertainty, people look to what others are doing to decide what to do themselves.

## Core idea

Popularity signals — reviews, follower counts, "1,000 people bought this", trending hashtags — substitute for independent judgment, especially under uncertainty.

## In digital manipulation

- Fake reviews, ratings, and testimonials.
- Botnets and fake engagement inflating perceived consensus.
- Disinformation campaigns manufacturing "grassroots" sentiment (astroturfing).
- Investment/crypto scams showing fake withdrawals and member testimonials.
