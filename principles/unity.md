# Unity

**Cialdini principle #7** — People are influenced by those they perceive as sharing an identity with them — "one of us".

## Core idea

Beyond mere liking, unity is about shared group identity: nationality, community, fandom, ideology, profession. "In-group" members get trust and benefit of the doubt by default.

## In digital manipulation

- Disinformation tailored to specific communities and identities.
- Scams exploiting religious, political, or professional group membership.
- Insider-rumor channels and group chats used to spread investment fraud.
- Targeted propaganda framing outsiders as threats to the group.
