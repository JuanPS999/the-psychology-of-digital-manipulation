# Scarcity

**Cialdini principle #2** — People assign more value to things that are rare, limited, or about to become unavailable.

## Core idea

Perceived scarcity (of time, quantity, or access) triggers urgency and fear of missing out, short-circuiting deliberate evaluation. Opponents of decision quality: countdown timers, "only 2 left", exclusive invites.

## In digital manipulation

- Fake countdown timers and "low stock" notices on e-commerce sites.
- Phishing that demands action "within 24 hours" or the account will be closed.
- Invite-only launches and artificial waitlists.
- Ransom notes with deadlines.
