# Principles (Cialdini)

Robert Cialdini's seven principles of persuasion. Each page describes the principle, how it appears in digital manipulation, and lists the cases that exploit it.

## Index

1. [Reciprocity](reciprocity.md) — we feel obliged to return favors.
2. [Scarcity](scarcity.md) — we value what is rare or about to become unavailable.
3. [Authority](authority.md) — we defer to perceived experts and authority figures.
4. [Commitment and consistency](commitment-and-consistency.md) — we strive to act consistently with prior commitments.
5. [Liking](liking.md) — we are more easily persuaded by people we like.
6. [Social proof](social-proof.md) — we look to others' behavior to decide our own.
7. [Unity](unity.md) — we are influenced by shared identity, "us" groups.
