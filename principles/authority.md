# Authority

**Cialdini principle #3** — People defer to perceived experts, officials, and authority figures.

## Core idea

Symbols of authority — titles, uniforms, credentials, official branding — reduce critical scrutiny. We obey instructions from figures who appear legitimate without verifying them.

## In digital manipulation

- Spear-phishing impersonating CEOs, IT departments, banks, or government agencies.
- Fake tech-support scams claiming to be from Microsoft/Apple.
- Deepfake voice/video of executives authorizing fraudulent transfers.
- Spoofed "verified" accounts and cloned websites with official branding.
