# Reciprocity

**Cialdini principle #1** — People feel obliged to return favors, gifts, or concessions, even uninvited ones.

## Core idea

When someone gives us something — information, a free sample, a discount, help — we feel a social debt. Manipulators exploit this by giving small "gifts" first, then making requests that feel disproportionate to what was given.

## In digital manipulation

- Free tools, trials, or content followed by aggressive conversion asks.
- Phishing emails offering a "gift" (prize, refund, free subscription) to lower defenses.
- Dark patterns that frame tracking consent as repayment for a free service.
