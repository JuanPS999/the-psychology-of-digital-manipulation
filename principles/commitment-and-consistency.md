# Commitment and consistency

**Cialdini principle #4** — People strive to appear consistent with their prior statements, positions, and small commitments.

## Core idea

Once we take a small stand or make a small commitment, internal and social pressures push us to behave consistently with it. Manipulators escalate: a tiny "yes" today makes a bigger "yes" tomorrow more likely (foot-in-the-door).

## In digital manipulation

- Multi-step scams that start with trivial actions (click, reply, small payment) and escalate.
- Dark-pattern funnels that get users to invest time ("sunk effort") before revealing costs.
- Fraud schemes (e.g., romance/pig-butchering) built on gradually escalating victim commitments.
