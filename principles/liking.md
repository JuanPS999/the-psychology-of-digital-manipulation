# Liking

**Cialdini principle #5** — People are more easily persuaded by those they like — because of similarity, compliments, attractiveness, or familiarity.

## Core idea

Rapport lowers defenses. Attackers invest in warmth, shared interests, flattery, and attractive personas to build trust before the exploitation begins.

## In digital manipulation

- Romance scams and pig-butchering: long rapport-building phases.
- Social media impersonation of friends and acquaintances.
- Influencer personas and parasocial trust used to promote scams.
- Phishing tailored to the target's interests and context (spear phishing).
